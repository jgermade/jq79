# What is open after PR #13

**Status:** a list, and the successor to
[`2026-08-25.open-after-the-oracle.md`](2026-08-25.open-after-the-oracle.md),
whose §1 closed (all three engines answered) and whose §7 first line is spent
(the baseline moved to 0.6.4). That file is not edited; this one replaces it as
the map. What the branch *did* is
[`2026-08-25.what-this-branch-did.md`](2026-08-25.what-this-branch-did.md).

Written to be picked up in a **new session, after
[PR #13](https://github.com/jgermade/jq79/pull/13) is merged** - so the first
thing to do is `git fetch origin main` and branch from it, not to continue the
branch this was written on.

Not a proposal. Each item says what it is, what evidence exists, and how big it
looks. Ordered by what I would do first, which is not by size.

---

## 0. The rule this branch earned, before anything below

**Write the sabotage before trusting the check.** Four checks in PR #13 turned
out unable to fail or unable to run, and three of the four were written by the
hand that wrote the thing they check. Break a new check on purpose, watch it go
red, then fix it. A green check nobody has seen fail is not evidence.

## 1. The component tag prefix - step 1 only

[`2026-08-25.component-tag-prefix.md`](2026-08-25.component-tag-prefix.md)

**The largest thing open, the only one with a plan already written, and the one
to start with.** `src/` is untouched by design.

The plan is in two steps and they should be two changes:

- **Step 1 - the rename and the resolution.** `<Circle />` becomes
  `<c79-circle :jq79-component="Circle">` in the pre-parse rewrite; resolution
  reads `node.component` instead of `findComponentKey(scope, node.tag)`;
  `<drop-area>` keeps the dashed path. **This is the whole of the fix** - the
  capture closes in both directions here, and nothing below it is needed for
  that.
- **Step 2 - the CSS rename and `display: contents`.** Rewriting `Circle { … }`
  to `c79-circle[data-jq79="…"] { … }`, and the wrapper becoming an addressable
  box. Its own plan, on top of a landed step 1.

Start with step 1 because it is verifiable with instruments that already exist:
the skeleton differential corpus, and the same probe method that measured the
collisions. Three concrete pieces of work it carries:

- **A close-tag pass.** `OPEN_TAG_RE` matches open tags only, which is fine
  today because the parser lowercases both ends to the same name. Rename one end
  and not the other and `<c79-circle>` gets closed by `</circle>`.
- **Two corpus entries change meaning.** The `<mychip>` entry exists to prove an
  undashed lowercase tag becomes a component when a key arrives later - that
  behaviour is being withdrawn. And a component in table-row position moves.
- **Docs.** `<Tr />` and `<mychip>` are documented behaviour; both go.

### The two decisions it is waiting on

- **Which version.** It withdraws `<mychip>` and moves a component out of
  table-row position, so **0.7 with a migration note**, not a patch. Nobody has
  said so out loud yet.
- **What scope stamp the wrapper carries** (step 2 only, not measured). "The
  child's" preserves today's behaviour - a parent's scoped CSS already cannot
  reach a child component's root. "The parent's" would be a new capability.

The third question - *does every instance get a wrapper, including single-root
and empty ones* - **is answered: always.** Reasoning and measurements in the
dated section at the bottom of the plan.

## 2. `:attrs` writes a dead attribute without a word

Unchanged from §2 of the previous list, and now the smallest useful thing in the
file. `:attrs="{ strokeWidth: 4 }"` on a foreign element writes an attribute SVG
ignores, as silently as `{ viewBox: box }` writes a good one. The oracle already
computes the signal - `strokeWidth` lowercased does not come back as
`strokeWidth`, so it is not a name the parser ever produces - and a warn costs
one lookup on a path that already runs per binding.

Still a decision, not just work: the argument that killed
[`2026-08-07.unknown-directive-warn.md`](2026-08-07.unknown-directive-warn.md)'s
modal case applies here too - a diagnostic aimed at a common mistake can be a
sign the feature should exist instead.

## 3. What is left of the unknown-directive warn

Unchanged from §3. Two survivors that want different instruments:

- **`:submit.prevent`** writes an attribute literally named `submit.prevent`
  holding the handler stringified. `plannableAttr` already computes the
  structural signal - a dotted `:` name belonging to no family - and it cannot
  fire on a legal binding. **Small and safe.**
- **`:iff`** renders `iff="true"` with no structural signal at all. It needs a
  near-miss table against `CONTROL_ATTRS`, which this library does not have and
  which has its own false positives to argue about. **A judgement nobody has
  made.**

## 4. The invisible wrapper - and it now overlaps §1

Unchanged in substance from §4: the warning shipped, the wrapper did not, and
the work is `boundsOf`, which resolves a fragment as
`{ first: node.firstChild!, last: node.lastChild! }` and breaks on an empty one.
`<template :slot>` sidesteps it by always inserting its own anchors.

**What is new is that §1 walks into the same ground.** A component that always
carries a `<c79-name>` has an element where it had a possibly-empty range, and
`boundsOf` could resolve the wrapper instead of a pair of comments. Do not solve
this one twice - if §1 lands, re-read this item before starting it.

## 5. One seam left of the two

- **`<annotation-xml>` stays off the clone path**, and takes its `<math>`
  ancestor with it, because `plannableNode` rejects a dashed tag and does not
  make the exception `mayUpgrade` now makes. Slower and correct. A page that
  renders one annotated formula pays nothing; nobody has one that renders a
  thousand.
- The other seam, **the capture asymmetry** (`Mi` in scope captures `<mi>`, a
  key arriving later does not), is what §1 removes. Left here only so that a
  reader of the previous list can see where it went.

## 6. Two measurement gaps, unchanged and still the right trade

- **`renders=8` never resolved.** Two attempts at the break-even curve gave
  +2.2%/46% and +8.5%/39%, both with a p25..p75 spanning ~55 points, because a
  chunk there is 480 renders and ~19ms. It needs a smaller chunk. Not needed for
  any answer so far; 1 and 100 bracket it.
- **The order axis is differential only.** It compares the two paths against
  each other and does not assert either matches a written-down expected order,
  so a change that reorders *both* identically still passes. The right trade for
  a differential test, and worth knowing it is the trade.

## 7. Housekeeping

- **The PR #13 description is stale.** It says "872 to 899"; the branch ended at
  **909**, and some inline tags were stripped to empty backticks. One edit, if
  the PR is still open when this is read.
- **The baseline moves again at the next release.** §7 of the previous list
  fired once already when 0.6.4 landed. Whatever release carries PR #13
  re-baselines the regression job again; a flat delta afterwards means the floor
  rose, not that nothing changed.
- **jsdom and Chromium disagree about a component inside `<select>`.** jsdom
  drops an unknown child, Chromium 151 keeps it (customizable-select parsing).
  Found while sizing §1, caused by nothing in this repo and fixed by nothing in
  it - but it means this repo's tests are not authoritative about that position.
- **`moveRangeAfter` still moves a reorder one node at a time.** `swapRows` is
  7.8ms. Still not worth it.
- **The stress harness is not in the repo.** The flake in the previous branch
  needed every core busy and three suites at once to reproduce, and that was a
  shell loop, not a script. If it happens again, that is the method: spin loops
  per core, then `npx vitest run` repeatedly, then read *which assertion* failed
  rather than which file.
- **`tests/vitePlugin.test.ts` writes to a fixed bundle path.** Two concurrent
  vitest processes overwrite it mid-import. A latent hazard rather than a flake -
  it cannot bite a single-process run, and CI is single-process.
- **Two files here are dated the day before they were written.**
  `2026-08-24.clone-path-coverage-check.md` and `2026-08-24.mathml.md`. House
  precedent, not a slip; recorded so a reader sorting by filename is not
  confused.

## 8. The part none of this touches

Unchanged and still honest: of the ~8ms of DOM, ~11ms of interpretation and
~30ms of effects per 1,000 rows in
[`2026-08-23.where-the-create-time-goes.md`](2026-08-23.where-the-create-time-goes.md),
the clone path took the middle third. The remaining ~30ms is one effect per
binding, and the two things priced against it - grouping a row's bindings, and
dropping `with` from expression evaluation - were both refused for good reasons.

`create1k` is ~33ms where Svelte is ~9ms. Closing that is a claim about what the
library is, not an optimization anybody has left.

## Update, 2026-08-25: §1 step 1 is implemented

The rename and the resolution are in `src/`; the record is a dated section at the
bottom of
[`2026-08-25.component-tag-prefix.md`](2026-08-25.component-tag-prefix.md).

- **§1 is half spent.** Step 1 - the rename, the close-tag pass, resolution off
  the stamp - landed with its three pieces of work: the close-tag pass, the two
  corpus entries that changed meaning, and the docs. Step 2 (the CSS rename,
  `display: contents`, the wrapper in the DOM) is untouched and still a proposal.
- **§1's first decision is still open.** Nobody has said "0.7 with a migration
  note" out loud, and the branch does not bump the version. The note has three
  lines now, not two - the third is that a capitalized tag no longer falls back
  to the native element of that name (`<DIV>`, and the `<A>` in the anti-recursion
  test), which this file's plan did not predict.
- **§1's second decision (the wrapper's scope stamp) is untouched**, because it
  belongs to step 2.
- **§4 is unchanged and still overlaps step 2, not step 1.** A resolved component
  still renders as a pair of comment anchors and no element, so `boundsOf` has
  nothing new to resolve yet.
- **§5's capture asymmetry is closed.** `Mi` in scope no longer captures `<mi>`,
  and a lowercase tag resolves to no component in either direction. The
  `<annotation-xml>` half of §5 is unchanged: it is still off the clone path.
- **What the rename paid for.** The clone path no longer asks whether a scope key
  captured one of its tags - `SkeletonPlan` dropped `tags` and the
  `findComponentKey` call per distinct tag went with it.
