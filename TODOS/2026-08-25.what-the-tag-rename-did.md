## Step 1: the parser can no longer resolve a component tag

```
<Circle />    ->  <c79-circle :jq79-component="Circle" />
</UserCard>   ->  </c79-user-card>
```

- The rewrite renames **every** capitalized tag, and a close-tag pass came with
  it - rename one end only and `</circle>` closes `<c79-circle>` and swallows
  what follows.
- `componentKeyOf` is the only place that decides what a tag resolves to, and it
  takes two spellings: the capitalized name off the stamp, and a dashed tag.
  **An undashed lowercase tag resolves to nothing, whatever is in scope.**
- The upgrade watch narrowed to those same two spellings.
- The clone path stopped asking whether a scope key captured one of its tags;
  `SkeletonPlan` dropped `tags` entirely. The first thing the rename *paid for*.
- `tagLabel(node)` keeps every diagnostic naming what the author wrote.

**Three behaviours withdrawn**, and the third was not predicted by the plan:

1. A component can no longer sit in table-row position - `<Row />` inside a
   `<tbody>` is foster-parented out of the table, like any non-`<tr>`.
2. `<mychip>` no longer becomes `MyChip` when the key arrives.
3. A capitalized tag that resolves to no component no longer falls back to the
   native element of that name. `<DIV>` throws where it rendered a div, and the
   anti-recursion test's inner `<A>` throws where it rendered an anchor.

## Step 2: a component is an element

```html
<div class="w"><c79-one data-c79-box><!--One-->…<!--/One--></c79-one></div>
```

- **Always** - one root, several, or none - so the shape of the tree does not
  depend on what the child rendered this pass.
- **Named after the component**, not the usage site: `<UserCard />` and
  `<user-card>` both render `<c79-user-card>`.
- **It carries the parent's scope stamp**, which is what makes a component's
  name work as a selector: `Chip { … }` becomes
  `c79-chip[data-jq79="<parent's hash>"]`.
- The CSS rename runs on the **source**, never on `rule.selectorText`, and only
  in selector position.
- `:where([data-c79-box]) { display: contents }` ships as the default, so any
  author rule outranks it.
- **No box inside `<svg>` or `<math>`.**

## What the benchmark said

Two runs, on two different runners - so the absolutes below are not comparable
across the columns and only the deltas mean anything. Neither run found a
regression.

**Step 1** (`70b57cd`, EPYC 7763):

| operation | base | head | delta | noise | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Create 1,000 rows | 35.3ms | 31.8ms | -10.6% | ±18.2% | inside the noise |
| Replace all 1,000 | 38.6ms | 37.4ms | -3.0% | ±3.0% | faster every round |
| Swap rows | 7.9ms | 7.6ms | -3.2% | ±11.4% | faster every round |
| Create 10,000 rows | 314ms | 296ms | -4.2% | ±9.9% | inside the noise |

**Step 2** (`1c01e44`, EPYC 9V74):

| operation | base | head | delta | noise | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Create 1,000 rows | 23.9ms | 23.9ms | -0.6% | ±7.3% | inside the noise |
| Replace all 1,000 | 29.5ms | 28.7ms | -2.1% | ±8.4% | faster every round |
| Remove a row | 4.3ms | 4.5ms | +7.5% | ±16.1% | inside the noise |
| Append 1,000 to 10,000 | 53.3ms | 54.9ms | +3.1% | ±4.5% | inside the noise |

**There is no result here, and step 2 could not have produced one.** The
benchmark app renders no nested components, so step 2 touched none of the code
that runs per row - its cost (one element and one `setAttribute` per component
instance) is invisible to every operation in the table by construction. Step 1's
-10.6% on `create1k` has one round at +7.5% and a spread of ±18.2% beside it,
which is the shape this repo already calls a non-result. The two "faster every
round" lines are supporting evidence and nothing more: four rounds agree on a
sign 12.5% of the time by chance.

The only claim either step makes about speed is that step 1 *removes* work from
the render path - one `findComponentKey` per distinct tag of every planned
subtree - and the benchmark is not sharp enough to see it.

## How it was checked

**Twelve sabotages, twelve reds**, before any check was believed - the rule
[`2026-08-25.open-after-pr-13.md`](2026-08-25.open-after-pr-13.md) §0 earned.
Five for step 1, seven for step 2, two of those beyond what the plan had listed
(renaming the whole stylesheet instead of selector positions, and treating a
string inside a selector as renamable text - the second was a real false
positive found by re-reading the diff, not by a test).

The honest reading of one of them: removing the rename entirely leaves five of
step 1's seven new tests **green**, because they check resolution, which the
stamp already carried. Only two of those tests are about the rename.

## What nobody has looked at

**No browser has rendered any of this.** The suite is jsdom, which has no
layout, so `display: contents`, the `:where()` specificity, the foster-parenting
of a component out of a table and the SVG exception are all reasoning from the
specifications with a jsdom-shaped test under them. The `engines` workflow runs
the SVG attribute oracle and nothing else. That is the first item in the open
list.
